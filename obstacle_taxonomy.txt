CRITICAL RULES, EXCLUSIONS & CLEARANCE REASONING:
====================================================================================================
1. TRAIN / ON-RAILS EXCLUSION:
   - Trains, locomotives, wagons, and trams ('on-rails') are STRICTLY EXCLUDED.
   - Prevents false alarms from oncoming trains on adjacent lines, parallel yard tracks, or own locomotive.

2. SENSOR & ILLUMINATION MODES:
   - 50% Daylight RGB + 50% Synthetic Active 850nm Near-Infrared (NIR) Night Vision.
   - Multi-mode adaptive atmospheric defogging (CLAHE, Dark Channel Prior, Fog Score).

3. THREE-TIER SPATIAL CLEARANCE REASONING (ATP INTERVENTION):
   - Ground Footprint polygon of each detected object is projected onto the BiSeNetV2 track mask:
     * 🔴 CRITICAL (In-Track):   Footprint touches drivable ballast / running rails -> Emergency Brake!
     * 🟡 WARNING (Near-Track): Footprint within lateral clearance envelope (<= 65px) -> Driver HUD Alert.
     * 🟢 CLEAR (Off-Track):    Footprint outside railway corridor (platform/roadway) -> Track Safe.
