CRITICAL RULES & EXCLUSIONS:
====================================================================================================
1. TRAIN / ON-RAILS EXCLUSION:
   - RailSem19 Label 16 ('on-rails' - trains, locomotives, wagons, trams) is STRICTLY EXCLUDED.
   - The detector will NEVER generate a bounding box on a train itself, preventing false alarms
     from oncoming trains on parallel tracks or yard wagons.

2. SENSOR & DAY/NIGHT AUGMENTATION:
   - 50% Daylight RGB + 50% Synthetic 850nm Active Infrared (NIR) pairs generated for every image.
   - Ensures robust detection under locomotive headlight beams, fog dehazing, and pitch-dark night.

3. THREE-TIER ATP HAZARD REASONING:
   - Detected Bounding Box Bottom Ground Footprint (F_k) is projected onto BiSeNetV2 Track Mask:
     * CRITICAL (In-Track): Footprint touches running rails / track gauge -> Kavach Emergency Brake
     * WARNING (Near-Track): Footprint inside warning buffer envelope -> Loco Pilot HUD Alert
     * SAFE (Off-Track): Footprint on adjacent platform/slope -> Logged as safe (No Alarm)
